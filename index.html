SpringRole

visit ↗

A live, production job-matching platform I built from scratch during my software engineering internship, now running with real users at springrole.io and handed off to a new team for continued development. Candidates swipe through AI-matched roles Tinder-style, run their resume through an AI CV analysis and skill-gap engine, and prep with mock interviews, all with light and dark mode support. Employers get an AI-assisted job posting flow, a curated credit-gated candidate database, a company profile with AI-generated benefits and office locations, and a Kanban-style hiring pipeline to track applicants from shortlist to offer. Built full-stack with Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) and Tailwind CSS on the frontend, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, and the OpenAI API on the backend, deployed on DigitalOcean App Platform.
